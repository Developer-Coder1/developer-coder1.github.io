TekkyTouch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

TekkyTouch is developed by Oxmena Services. Contact us at oxmenaservices@gmail.com

What data we collect

We collect the following information when you use TekkyTouch:

  • Email address (for account creation)
  • Name/username
  • Sport, position, and skill level
  • XP points, rank progress, and training streak
  • Drill history (the drills you have completed)
  • App usage information, such as when a drill is started, completed, or skipped, to help us improve the app

How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To personalise your training drills
  • To display your rank on the leaderboard (Premium users)
  • To save your progress across sessions
  • To send you a welcome email when you join
  • To understand how the app is used and fix problems

Third party services

We use the following third party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, data storage, and app analytics
  • Gemini AI (Google) — generating personalised training drills
  • YouTube API Services (Google) — finding and showing drill videos
  • Google Play Billing — handling Pro subscriptions. We never see your payment details.
  • Resend — sending our welcome email

YouTube videos

TekkyTouch uses YouTube API Services to show videos that demonstrate drills. These videos are made by their creators and play from YouTube inside the app. By watching them, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

When you choose to watch a video, we send a short search for that drill (for example, "netball chest pass drill") to YouTube. We do not send your name, email, or any other personal information to YouTube.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. If you stop using TekkyTouch, your data is retained in case you return, unless you request deletion as described below.

Account & Data Deletion

To delete your TekkyTouch account and all associated data, email oxmenaservices@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account."

We will delete your account, including your email, name, sport, position, skill level, XP, and drill history, within 30 days of your request. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children

TekkyTouch is not intended for children under 13. Users must be 13 or older to create an account.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app means you accept any changes.
